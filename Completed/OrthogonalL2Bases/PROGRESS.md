## OrthogonalL2Bases: 2026-06-28 to 2026-08-03 (`4893c9f` to `11ef09d`)

Two summits landed. The Hermite functions are now a Hilbert basis of `L²(ℝ)` over any `RCLike` scalar field ([`hermiteHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/HilbertBasis.html#EpsilonEridani.hermiteHilbertBasis)), and the Chebyshev polynomials `Tₙ` are a Hilbert basis of `L²` for Mathlib's Chebyshev measure ([`chebyshevTHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/HilbertBasis.html#EpsilonEridani.chebyshevTHilbertBasis)). Each ships the element-level lemma identifying its vectors with the normalized family, so neither is a bare separability statement, and both complete through one mechanism: a finite measure on `ℝ` carrying a single finite exponential moment is determined by its polynomial moments ([`Measure.ext_of_forall_integral_pow_eq_of_exists_integrable_exp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Probability/Moments/Determinacy.html#EpsilonEridani.Measure.ext_of_forall_integral_pow_eq_of_exists_integrable_exp), EpsilonEridani#1028). That proof runs through characteristic functions and analyticity of the complex moment generating function on a strip, not the roadmap's proposed entire Fourier integral.

The family-agnostic layer beneath both is in place, in both normalizations: `√w`-envelope functions on `L²(μ)` and bare polynomials on the weighted measure, exchanged by multiplication by `√w` ([`weightL2Isometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/MeasureTheory/Function/WeightL2Isometry.html#EpsilonEridani.weightL2Isometry)) and transport of a Hilbert basis along a linear isometric equivalence ([`HilbertBasis.mapₗᵢ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HilbertBasisMap.html#HilbertBasis.mapₗᵢ)). Product bases followed for two factors and for finite products ([`prodHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Product.html#EpsilonEridani.prodHilbertBasis), [`piHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Pi.html#EpsilonEridani.piHilbertBasis)); the work there is completeness, by a Dynkin argument carrying vanishing integrals on rectangles to vanishing integrals on every finite-measure set. Composed with Part A this gives the multidimensional bases [`hermiteFunctionPiBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/PiBasis.html#EpsilonEridani.hermiteFunctionPiBasis) and [`gaussianHermitePiBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Hermite/PiBasis.html#EpsilonEridani.gaussianHermitePiBasis).

Around the summits: Parseval and the Hermite expansion of an arbitrary `L²` function ([`hasSum_hermiteFunctionLp_expansion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Parseval.html#EpsilonEridani.hasSum_hermiteFunctionLp_expansion)); the eigenrelation `𝓕 Φₙ = (-i)ⁿ Φₙ`, stated for the `√(2π)`-rescaled family that Mathlib's Fourier convention needs ([`fourier_twoPiHermiteFunction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier.html#EpsilonEridani.fourier_twoPiHermiteFunction)); the cosine basis of `L²((0, π])` pulled back along `x = cos θ` ([`chebyshevCosineHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/Cosine/HilbertBasis.html#EpsilonEridani.chebyshevCosineHilbertBasis)); and the Hermite generating function and Schwartz-space ladder relations.
