## QuarkoniaAndExotics: 2026-06-09 to 2026-11-26 (`876857b` to `a9971be`)

The first matching theorem for NRQCD has landed: [uniqueness of the matching coefficients](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/Matching.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.PowerCounting.IsMatching.eqOn_truncation) says that if, at every total degree up to the working order, the matrix of velocity-stripped matrix elements has full column rank, then two coefficient assignments reproducing the same full-theory amplitudes agree on every retained operator, and a [sharper form](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/Matching.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.PowerCounting.IsMatching.forall_eqOn_truncation_iff) shows the rank condition is also necessary (EpsilonEridani#248). The [matching condition](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/Matching.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.PowerCounting.IsMatching) carries an explicit `O(ε^(n+1))` remainder, phrased through a general notion of [approximation to a given order](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/Analysis/Asymptotics/ApproximatesToOrder.html#EpsilonEridani.ApproximatesToOrder) that the TMD power-correction code now shares. Existence of matching coefficients and regulator independence are not yet addressed.

Underneath sits the Layer 0 grading: a [velocity power counting](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/PowerCounting.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.PowerCounting) on heavy-quark bilinears, with the theorem that [each truncation is finite](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/PowerCounting.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.finite_totalDegree_compositeDeg_le) and a characterisation of [exactly when](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/PowerCounting.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.finite_totalDegree_compositeDeg_le_iff) that holds. The [scale hierarchy](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/Scales.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.HeavyQuarkScales) is now data, with `0 < v < 1`, the weakly and strongly coupled regimes shown exclusive, and a proof that [Coulombic scaling is a genuine extra assumption](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/Scales.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.HeavyQuarkScales.exists_not_coulombicScaling), not a consequence of `0 < E < p < m`.

On the spectrum side, the [singlet and octet colour projectors](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/ColorDecomposition.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.completeOrthogonalIdempotents_colorSingletProj_colorOctetProj) of a quark-antiquark pair are built from the `su(N)` generators and proved to be complete orthogonal idempotents, with traces `1` and `N² − 1` and Casimir values `0` and `N`. The [attainable `J^{PC}`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/QuantumNumbers.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.QuantumNumbers.qqbarAttainable_iff) of a pair are characterised in closed form and decidably, and the [exotic assignments with `J ≤ 3`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Quarkonium/NRQCD/QuantumNumbers.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Quarkonium.QuantumNumbers.not_qqbarAttainable_and_J_le_three_iff) come out as exactly `0^{−−}`, `0^{+−}`, `1^{−+}`, `2^{+−}`, `3^{−+}`. The parity and charge-conjugation formulas are still the definition of the pair's quantum numbers rather than derived from the bilinears.
