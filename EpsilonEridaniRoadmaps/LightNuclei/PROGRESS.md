## LightNuclei: 2026-06-09 to 2026-11-25 (`84b20f6` to `02b225d`)

The special functions of Layer 0 arrived. The [Legendre polynomials](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Legendre/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.Polynomial.legendre), defined by Bonnet's recurrence, now come with their [orthogonality relation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Legendre/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.Polynomial.integral_legendre_mul_legendre) on [-1, 1] with normalisation 2/(2n+1), [Legendre's differential equation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Legendre/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.Polynomial.derivative_one_sub_X_sq_mul_derivative_legendre) in Sturm–Liouville form, and their degree, parity and endpoint values (EpsilonEridani#77). The [spherical Bessel functions of the first kind](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/SphericalBessel/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.sphericalBesselJ) are built from the Rayleigh formula on the whole real line. They come with the [spherical Bessel equation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/SphericalBessel/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.sphericalBesselJ_equation), the three-term recurrence, the raising and lowering ladder relations, and the [leading behaviour at the origin](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/SphericalBessel/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.tendsto_sphericalBesselJ_div_pow_nhdsNE_zero) with its double-factorial coefficient. The [second kind](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/SphericalBessel/SecondKind.html#EpsilonEridani.sphericalBesselY) followed, with its divergence at the origin and the [Wronskian of the two kinds](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/SphericalBessel/SecondKind.html#EpsilonEridani.wronskian_sphericalBesselJ_sphericalBesselY), equal to x⁻².

The first nuclear file is the spin density matrix of a spin-one target from Layer 1. It defines [vector](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Nuclei/Light/SpinDensityMatrix.html#EpsilonEridani.Spin.vectorPolarization) and [tensor polarisation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Nuclei/Light/SpinDensityMatrix.html#EpsilonEridani.Spin.tensorPolarization) and proves that every 3×3 matrix is [recovered from its trace, vector and tensor parts](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Nuclei/Light/SpinDensityMatrix.html#EpsilonEridani.Spin.spinOneMatrix_decomposition). It also shows that [a spin-one-half target has no tensor polarisation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Nuclei/Light/SpinDensityMatrix.html#EpsilonEridani.Spin.tensorPolarization_spinHalfMatrix) and proves the positivity bounds, including [3|Pₖ| ≤ 2 + Pₖₖ](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Nuclei/Light/SpinDensityMatrix.html#EpsilonEridani.Spin.three_mul_norm_vectorPolarization_spinOneMatrix_le). The decomposition is done by hand in the Cartesian basis, not as an instance of Clebsch-Gordan as the roadmap envisages.

Still missing from Layer 0: large-argument asymptotics, the bound |Pₙ| ≤ 1, completeness, spherical harmonics, and the partial-wave expansion. The facts listing for this window is truncated, so some smaller lemmas may not be mentioned here.
