## ElectroweakAndBSM: 2026-06-09 to 2026-11-02 (`cc5fa73` to `f8d9db7`)

The first work on this roadmap went into the root of Layer 0, the gauge structure. The headline is a complete [classification of anomaly-free hypercharge assignments](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/PVES/Electroweak/Representation.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.PVES.Electroweak.GenerationAssignments.isAnomalyFree_iff_exists_eq_smul_standardModel_or_swapQuarkSinglets_or_mk_zero_neg) for one generation (EpsilonEridani#75). It says that the four anomaly conditions hold exactly for rescalings of the Standard Model hypercharges, for the same rescalings with the two right-handed quark singlets swapped, and for a degenerate one-parameter family `(0, t, -t, 0, 0)`. That is more honest than the README's "unique up to scale", which turns out to need these two exceptions. The anomaly conditions are also shown to match Physlib's one-family conditions.

The electric charge is now derived rather than declared. [Electric charge is the unbroken generator](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/PVES/Electroweak/ElectricCharge.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.PVES.Electroweak.doubletStabilizer_higgsVacuum): the generators of `su(2) ⊕ u(1)` that annihilate the Higgs vacuum are exactly the multiples of one generator. That generator [acts on a doublet as `T³ + Y/2`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/PVES/Electroweak/ElectricCharge.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.PVES.Electroweak.doubletAction_electricChargeGenerator) and on a singlet as `Y/2`. The proof uses explicit matrices rather than the TauCeti weight machinery the README suggested. No lemma in this window lists the physical charges of the five multiplets.

Neutral-gauge mixing is done in both directions. The neutral mass matrix has [exactly one massless combination](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/PVES/Electroweak/Mixing.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.PVES.Electroweak.neutralMassMatrix_mulVec_eq_zero_iff). A rotation diagonalises it with a massless photon [if and only if its angle is `arctan(g'/g)`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/PVES/Electroweak/Mixing.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.PVES.Electroweak.rotation_conj_neutralMassMatrix_eq_diagonal_iff), so the "diagonalising" and "coupling-ratio" definitions of the [weak mixing angle](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/PVES/Electroweak/Mixing.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.PVES.Electroweak.weakMixingAngle) agree. The existing parameter record's `sin²θ_W` field is tied to this angle at tree level. Supporting work added plane rotation matrices and a small `su(n)` membership lemma.
