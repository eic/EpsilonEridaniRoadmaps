## MesonStructure: 2026-06-09 to 2026-11-15 (`480945f` to `2850fc3`)

The headline is the [orthogonality relation for the Gegenbauer polynomials of index 3/2](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Gegenbauer/Measure.html#EpsilonEridani.integral_gegenbauerThreeHalves_mul_gegenbauerThreeHalves): against the weight 1 − x² on [−1, 1], distinct polynomials integrate to zero, and the squared norm of the n-th is 2(n+1)(n+2)/(2n+3). These polynomials are the eigenbasis that ERBL evolution will later be diagonalised in. The family itself is [defined by its three-term recurrence](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Gegenbauer/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.gegenbauerThreeHalves), with its degree, leading coefficient, value at 1 and [parity](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Gegenbauer/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.gegenbauerThreeHalves_comp_neg_X). Orthogonality is proved from [the Gegenbauer differential equation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/SpecialFunctions/Gegenbauer/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.one_sub_X_sq_mul_derivative_derivative_gegenbauerThreeHalves) in Sturm–Liouville form, together with a general lemma that [such operators are symmetric](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Mathematics/Calculus/Polynomial.html#Polynomial.integral_derivative_mul_derivative_mul_comm) when the weight vanishes at the endpoints. The moments, the isometry onto ℓ² and completeness are still to come.

On the physics side, a [pseudoscalar meson](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Meson/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.Particles.Meson.PseudoscalarMeson) is now a piece of data: mass, decay constant, charge and valence pair. It comes with charge conjugation, net valence numbers, and the charged pions and kaons with Particle Data Group values. Symmetry breaking is a separate structure, [FlavorBreaking](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Meson/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.Particles.Meson.FlavorBreaking), whose isospin and SU(3) limits are the propositions that its mass differences vanish. The isospin limit is [strictly weaker](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Meson/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.Particles.Meson.FlavorBreaking.exists_isIsospinLimit_not_isSU3Limit). The neutral pion is not yet defined.

Generic parton densities gained [charge conjugation](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Parton/PDF/ChargeConjugation.html#EpsilonEridani.Particles.Parton.PDF.chargeConj) and a [valence number](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/Particles/Parton/PDF/ChargeConjugation.html#EpsilonEridani.Particles.Parton.PDF.valenceNumber). Conjugation preserves the density assumptions and the summed moments, and it negates every valence number. This is the machinery the pion isospin and charge-conjugation relations will be stated with, but none of those relations has landed yet.
