## InclusiveStructureFunctions: 2026-06-11 to 2026-11-16 (`4391ad1` to `a41428b`)

The headline of this window is positivity of the structure functions, derived from a positive-semidefinite helicity matrix rather than assumed. From such a matrix the project now proves [`F₁ ≥ 0`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.F1_nonneg), the target-mass-exact [`F_L ≥ 0`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.FLExact_nonneg) and the [parity-odd bound](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.sqrt_mul_abs_F3_le_two_mul_F1) `√(1 + γ²) |F₃| ≤ 2 F₁`. It goes past the roadmap with a [completeness theorem](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.exists_isHelicityMatrixOf_iff): those two bounds are exactly the condition for a triple of structure functions to come from some positive-semidefinite matrix. The window also shows that the longitudinal bound is [strictly weaker than Callan–Gross](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.exists_isHelicityMatrixOf_and_not_isCallanGross), and that the inclusive cross section is [non-negative](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.dSigmaDxDQ2_nonneg) throughout the physical region (EpsilonEridani#131).

Underneath this, the [inclusive cross section](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/CrossSection.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.crossSectionBracket) was defined with its `Y₊`, `Y₋`, `y²` structure, exact in the target mass. It [reduces to the existing leading-order formula](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/CrossSection.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.dSigmaDxDQ2_eq_loNCdSigma), and the project proves that [reduced cross sections at three distinct inelasticities determine `F₁`, `F₂`, `F₃` uniquely](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/CrossSection.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.existsUnique_reducedCrossSection_eq). On the tensor side, the [target-mass-exact `F_L`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Tensors/Longitudinal.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Tensors.Longitudinal.FLExact) is shown to be [the longitudinal absorption itself](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Tensors/Longitudinal.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Tensors.Longitudinal.two_xBj_mul_apply_pTransverse). The [vector/axial-vector leptonic tensor](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Tensors/Basic.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Tensors.Leptonic.lMuNuVA) arrived split into its symmetric part and its parity-odd `ε(·, ·, k, k')` part, and is conserved for massless leptons.

Two things are still missing. Nobody has yet contracted the leptonic tensor with the hadronic one: the cross-section bracket is defined directly from the structure functions instead. And the parity-odd half of the hadronic tensor decomposition, which is what pins down `F₃`, hasn't been started.
