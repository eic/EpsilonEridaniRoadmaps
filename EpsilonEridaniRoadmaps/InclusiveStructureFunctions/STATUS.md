### Named results

- **Completeness of the positivity bounds**: at positive `x`, a triple `(F₁, F₂, F₃)` comes from a positive-semidefinite helicity matrix exactly when `√(1 + γ²) |F₃| ≤ 2 F₁` and `F_L^{exact} ≥ 0`. A diagonal matrix then suffices. ([`exists_isHelicityMatrixOf_iff`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.exists_isHelicityMatrixOf_iff))

It comes alongside [`F₁ ≥ 0`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.F1_nonneg) and [`F_L^{exact} ≥ 0`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.FLExact_nonneg). ([`sqrt_mul_abs_F3_le_two_mul_F1`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/Positivity.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.IsHelicityMatrixOf.sqrt_mul_abs_F3_le_two_mul_F1))
- **Separation of the structure functions**: at fixed `(x, Q²)`, reduced cross sections measured at three distinct inelasticities determine exactly one triple `(F₁, F₂, F₃)`, because the matrix of inelasticity coefficients has a Vandermonde determinant. ([`existsUnique_reducedCrossSection_eq`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/CrossSection.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.existsUnique_reducedCrossSection_eq))
- **`F_L^{exact}` is the longitudinal absorption**: contracting a decomposed hadronic tensor into the longitudinal direction gives `2 x W(p_T, p_T) = (p_T · p_T) F_L^{exact}`, with nothing left over. ([`two_xBj_mul_apply_pTransverse`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Tensors/Longitudinal.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Tensors.Longitudinal.two_xBj_mul_apply_pTransverse))

([`dSigmaDxDQ2_eq_loNCdSigma`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/CrossSection.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.dSigmaDxDQ2_eq_loNCdSigma))

## The frontier

- **The contraction theorem (Layer 1.1)**: contract `lMuNuVA` with a decomposed hadronic tensor, prove that the symmetric–antisymmetric cross terms vanish, and recover `crossSectionBracket`. The antisymmetric–antisymmetric term needs the parity-odd hadronic form from Layer 0.2. Separately, the README's target `Y₊ ≥ 2 Y₋` on `0 ≤ y ≤ 1` is false for `y > 1 − 1/√3`. The project proved [`Y₋ ≤ Y₊`](https://eic.github.io/EpsilonEridani/docs/EpsilonEridani/QFT/Scattering/DIS/Inclusive/CrossSection.html#EpsilonEridani.QFT.Scattering.DIS.Inclusive.Yminus_le_Yplus) and `Y₋ ≤ 2y` instead, and the README should be corrected.
- **The parity-odd decomposition (Layer 0.2–0.3)**: show that the antisymmetric, conserved, covariant part of `W` is a single multiple of `ε(·, ·, p, q)`, which needs a four-dimensional `V`. Then build that form from the Levi-Civita volume form, together with a four-dimensional witness in which `F₃ ≠ 0`. This is acceptance example 1.
- **Dimensionless structure functions (Layer 0.4)**: the bundled `StructureFunctions` record, and proved identities relating `W₁`, `W₂`, `W₃` to `F₁`, `F₂`, `F₃`.
- **The spin-half elastic channel and Callan–Gross (Layer 2)**: the parity-odd `ElasticChannelOdd`, the spin-half inhabitant, and a scalar-parton channel with `F_L ≠ 0` (acceptance examples 5–7).
- **Sum rules (Layer 3)**: Adler, Gross–Llewellyn Smith and Gottfried need the Layer 2 flavour decompositions first. Layers 4 and 5 sit behind them.
